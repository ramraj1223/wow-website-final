Frequently Asked Questions

Will the final price change once work actually begins?

No. You get a single, fixed-price contract. Unless you explicitly request a scope change during the build, your price will not go up by a single dollar. No mid-project price spikes, no surprise fees.

How long will my bathroom remodel take?

Remodels typically take 7 to 21 working days based on size and complexity (express projects using ready-made panels can take as few as 5 days). We agree on a firm completion date upfront and lock it into your contract, backed by our Time Guarantee: if we go past that date, we pay you $100 for every single day delayed.

Are you licensed, insured, and backed by a warranty?

Yes. We are fully licensed with the City of Chicago and carry full insurance for all projects. Every job is directly supervised by our team and backed by a 1-Year Workmanship Warranty for total peace of mind.

What happens during the free in-person estimate? Will I be pressured to buy?

Zero high-pressure sales tactics. We visit your home, evaluate your space, listen to your goals, and take precise measurements. We deliver an exact, honest quote within 24 hours so you can decide on your own timeline.

How disruptive will the renovation be to my daily routine?

We protect your home like it's our own. We set up floor protection, seal off the work area to minimize dust travel, clean up daily before leaving, and keep your home safe and functional throughout the project.
